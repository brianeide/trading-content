## Question

Experienced derivatives trader interview question: 

Dr. Eifert is usually skeptical of selling puts. What is the trick Buffet pulled off when selling puts that completely gets around all Dr. Eifert's arguments and made it a brilliant trade that ran over the banks he traded with?

## Answer

https://x.com/bennpeifert/status/1882450316291125700 | https://twitter-thread.com/t/1882450316291125700

okay! some people knew about this in the replies but many didn't. 

the massively important thing here is that Buffet and team negotiated NO VARIATION MARGIN on these 10-15 year European put sales! literally unheard of and will never happen again

normally if you do some long-dated trade like this because you like the terminal payoff, it can still be very painful because the trade can move against you (like in 2008) and you have to post huge collateral against your mark to market loss

and this ties up tons of capital and impairs your abillity to take advantage of other opportunities in a very distressed market

Buffet and team argued that they had an impeccable credit rating and hey, they were totally good for it, no worries man, no need for all that formality of wiring money back and forth every day

This meant they could just happily sit on these 10+ year positions that ware almost guaranteed to work out on a hold to maturity basis, and not care at all what happened in between. They pre-empted essentially all the danger of a long term short tails position

They got paid handsomely for these trades because they were fulfilling buying demand from variable insurance companies in the wake of a quiet regulatory change in 2006 called C3 Phase 2 (thanks @Michael Green )

Regulators got concerned about the risk to variable annuity providers in a long term bear market and incentivized them to buy 10+ year equity index puts. Wall Street needed a seller and they called Buffet

relatively shortly after these trades, some of the banks involved did a more proper quantitative analysis of the value of the financing option they'd implicitly sold to Buffet was, and completely freaked out, massive error

the smarter banks tried to unload this risk on the ones who hadn't figured it out

and one of them that i know of changed their trade booking system to make it impossible to book a trade with no variation margin

defining some OTC derivatives terms:
- initial margin: cash a client posts to a bank up front to collateralize a derivatives trade, agreed at trade time
- variation margin: daily posting of mark to market pnl back and forth between counterparties. you don't just wait until the maturity of a trade and then settle all the cash flows, you mark it every day and settle daily pnl, this means counterparty risk is not on the whole notional of the trade, only on sudden PNL since last margin payment
