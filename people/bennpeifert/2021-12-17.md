# Junior derivatives trader interview question

https://x.com/bennpeifert/status/1471964060463759362 | https://twitter-thread.com/t/1471964060463759362

Friday junior derivatives trader interview question. Revisiting a theme we've done before but people struggle with a lot so we gonna do again.

S&P 1-month implied volatility is 16. The slope of the skew curve is -1 point per 1%, e.g. the 99% strike trades at a 1 vol point premium to the 100% strike.

You sell the ATM straddle. It has 20 bps of vega and zero black-scholes delta.
Tomorrow, the S&P rises 1%. The new at-the-money implied volatility is 15.5; the whole floating-strike vol curve for your maturity fell by 0.5 points.

What is your PNL?
